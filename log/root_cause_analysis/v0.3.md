### Systemic causes

- **Weak senior model.** gpt-5.4-mini scored 0/5 here and again in the v0.4.0 conversational loop;
  GLM-5.3 in the same loop scored 2/5. Model choice, not the loop, was the limiting factor.
- **Status outranked evidence.** `solved` beat `partial` regardless of confidence or whether the
  worker had searched the feed the question names.
- **Field manifest without types.** `agent/botsv3_fields.json` listed epoch and display-string time
  fields side by side with no types or examples, inviting the wrong pick.
