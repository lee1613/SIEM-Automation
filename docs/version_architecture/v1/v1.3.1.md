## The one-line hypothesis

Replace the `planner → executor → joiner → REPLAN` loop with a **bounded conversation**:
a small number of question-scoped seniors stay alive, report often against a standard
template, and SH steers them — instead of being killed and having their replacements
re-briefed from zero.

## Two change categories

Every change in v1.3.1 is classified into exactly one of these, and the classification is
recorded in the same turn as the change.

**GENERAL** — lands on `main`, survives a failed experiment. A fix or improvement that
would be right regardless of which orchestrator is running: shared-module bugs, logging,
the usage tracker, the Splunk client, the worker graph, documentation.

**EXPERIMENT** — lives only on `exp/v1.3.1-conversational`. The conversation loop, the
five route schemas, the report template, the thread log, the context tracker, and the
deletion of the joiner.

**Exit condition.** If the experiment fails, `git branch -D exp/v1.3.1-conversational` is
the entire revert — general changes are already on `main` and are untouched by it.

## Design summary

Detail lives in the spec; this is the shape.

**Loop.** SH spawns one senior. Each senior works up to 8 iterations, then emits a
templated markdown report. SH reads the whole parallel wave in **one** turn and issues one
route per senior. No joiner node, no `JOINER_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, no `REPLAN:` parse, no
`MAX_PLAN_ROUNDS`.

**Boundary rule, in both prompts.** *SH speaks in constraints and goals; the senior speaks
in evidence and SPL.* SH owns `scope_change` and nothing below it — that is how it can act
on a `NOT_FOUND` without writing a query, which is the job it cannot do.

**Five routes:** `SPAWN`, `RETIRE`, `COMMAND{continue|retry|critic}`, `CLARIFY` (1-3
questions, answered from memory, no tools, does not consume a round), `ANSWER` (keeps the
existing grounding gate).

**Seniors** are question-scoped, max 3 spawns per question, all retired at question end.
Spawning is demand-driven: SH starts with one and must justify each additional senior.
An `api_failed` or `runaway` retirement refunds its spawn slot.

**Budgets** key off `base_points`:

| Tier | Seniors | Rounds | Iters/round | Ceiling/senior | Question ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 pt | 1 | 3 | 8 | 24 | 24 |
| 500 pt | 2 | 5 | 8 | 40 | 80 |
| 1000 pt | 3 | 8 | 8 | 64 | 192 |

**Known risk:** the 1000-pt ceiling is 2.4x the 80 senior-iterations measured today. It is
a ceiling, not a schedule, and early retirement is what is supposed to keep actuals far
below it — so the run instruments actual-vs-ceiling per question. If actuals track the
ceiling, the experiment has failed on cost regardless of score.

**Artifacts per question:** `conversation.md` (group-chat transcript),
`reports/senior_<n>_round_<r>.md`, `handoffs/senior_<n>_handoff.md`.

**Context management** is predictive and checked at round start: compact when
`current + 8 x mean_tokens_per_iteration` would exceed 80% of the window; alert the
operator at 70%. Compaction needs no new prompt — the senior's latest report is already
its compressed state.

## Success criteria

Measured on a 5-question smoke test (`Q216 Q217 Q224 Q328 Q329`) against
`test_20260915_174228`:

1. **Score** beats 0/5.
2. **Cost per question** stays within ~2x of $0.272.
3. **Actual senior-iterations** track the 80 baseline, not the 192 ceiling.
4. Qualitatively: `conversation.md` shows SH actually steering — scope changes, critics,
   retirements — not rubber-stamping every report with `continue`.

Per the active cost-limited-runs policy, **no full run without explicit user go-ahead.**

## Future work recorded during this cycle

**A difficulty router.** Every budget above keys off `base_points`, which BOTSv3 hands us
free. Real-world questions carry no such label, so this scheme does not transfer off BOTS
without something that classifies difficulty first. Candidates, cheapest first: a feature
heuristic (entity count, whether a feed is named, scalar vs list answer, cross-sourcetype
join required); embedding nearest-neighbour against the 56 labelled BOTSv3 questions; or a
cheap LLM classifier returning a tier plus a rationale. The router only has to be
approximately right — the tier sets a ceiling, and demand-driven spawning already keeps
easy questions cheap inside a generous one.

**Pruning the technique axis.** `hunter` and `content` in `agent/v1/specialists.py` may be
redundant once SH issues a directive every 8 iterations; `metrics` encodes a hard rule SH
cannot enforce mid-round and likely stays. Decide from the first run's `conversation.md`,
not from argument.
